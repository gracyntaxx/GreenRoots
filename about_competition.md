# 1. About the Hackathon

The PayPal AI Hackathon is a global online hackathon inviting developers, designers, founders, students, and builders to explore new possibilities by combining PayPal's payment infrastructure with Artificial Intelligence.

PayPal already facilitates financial transactions for millions of individuals and businesses worldwide. This hackathon challenges participants to imagine what happens when AI becomes an integral part of financial interactions.

Participants can develop:

- AI-powered agents.
- Intelligent payment experiences.
- Automated financial workflows.
- AI-powered business tools.
- New ways to pay and receive money.
- Agentic commerce applications.
- Consumer-focused financial applications.
- Social-impact and financial-inclusion solutions.

There are no predefined problem statements or fixed tracks.

**Fundamental requirement: Every project must meaningfully use BOTH PayPal and AI.**

A project that merely mentions PayPal or uses AI as an unrelated chatbot will not satisfy the central requirement.

## 1.1 Hackathon Objectives

1. Build an innovative application using PayPal and AI.
2. Explore new ways for people, businesses, and AI agents to interact with money.
3. Experiment with PayPal APIs, SDKs, AI tools, and agentic workflows.
4. Create a functional prototype or proof of concept.
5. Demonstrate a real-world problem and a practical solution.
6. Present the project to PayPal judges, developers, and the wider hackathon community.

The project must meaningfully integrate at least one PayPal developer platform technology, API, SDK, product, or developer capability.

## 4.2 Artificial Intelligence Integration

The project must meaningfully incorporate an AI tool, model, or platform.

Participants can choose any AI technology.

Possible applications include:

- AI-powered recommendations.

## 6.2 Sandbox Setup

Recommended initial setup:

1. Visit https://developer.paypal.com/.
2. Sign in using an existing PayPal account or create an account.
3. Open the PayPal Developer Dashboard.
4. Navigate to the Apps & Credentials section.
5. Create a sandbox application.
6. Obtain the sandbox Client ID and Secret.
7. Configure sandbox buyer and business accounts as required.
8. Integrate the sandbox credentials into the application.
9. Test the complete payment workflow.
10. Verify that transactions are successfully processed in the sandbox.

**Security requirements:**

- Never expose the PayPal Secret in frontend code.
- Store sensitive credentials in environment variables.
- Never commit `.env` files containing secrets.
- Use sandbox credentials for development and judging.
- Clearly document the required environment variables.

# 10. Judging Process and Criteria

Judging takes place in two stages.

## Stage 1: Initial Viability Assessment

This is a pass/fail evaluation.

Judges assess whether:

- The project reasonably fits the hackathon theme.
- The project meaningfully uses PayPal APIs or SDKs.
- The project meaningfully incorporates AI.
- The proposed application is viable.

Projects must pass this stage to proceed to the next stage.

## Stage 2: Detailed Evaluation

All five criteria are equally weighted.

| Criterion | What Judges Evaluate |
|---|---|
| Technological Implementation | Quality of PayPal integration, AI implementation, technical complexity, and working functionality |
| Design | Completeness, usability, coherence, and overall product experience |
| Potential Impact | Real-world problem, target audience, relevance, and practical usefulness |
| Innovation / Idea | Creativity, originality, and differentiation from existing solutions |
| Presentation | Demo quality, clarity of pitch, end-to-end functionality, and communication |

### 10.1 Technological Implementation

Demonstrate:

- Meaningful PayPal integration.
- Meaningful AI implementation.
- Functional and non-trivial features.
- Reliable application execution.
- Proper API and SDK usage.

### 10.2 Design

Demonstrate:

- A complete product experience.
- Clear navigation.
- Good UI/UX.
- Consistent design.
- An understandable user workflow.

### 10.3 Potential Impact

Explain:

- Who experiences the problem.
- Why the problem matters.
- How the application addresses it.
- Why the solution is practically useful.
- Its potential for adoption and scalability.

### 10.4 Innovation

Demonstrate:

- Original thinking.
- A differentiated solution.
- Creative use of AI and PayPal.
- A new or improved financial experience.

### 10.5 Presentation

Demonstrate:

- A clear problem statement.
- A concise explanation of the solution.
- A functional end-to-end demo.
- PayPal and AI working together.
- A compelling explanation of the project's value.

**All five criteria carry equal weight during Stage 2.**
